# Phase 4 — personalized matching

Phase 4 adds candidate-owned compatibility to the shared vacancy catalogue. The legacy owner's score, immediate Telegram alert, daily digest and v1 workspace retain their existing admission and notification rules. Personalized results never call Telegram or alter global scores. Production release remains NO-GO without separate approval; no Phase 5 implementation is included.

## Facts, eligibility and scoring

`VacancyFactExtractor` stores immutable neutral source facts, requirements, source quotations and provenance. Retained provider inputs exclude raw response blobs and survive later bootstrap. Required, preferred and unspecified modality are distinct. Unsupported mandatory clauses, alternatives, negation or truncated extraction retain an UNKNOWN check. The extractor supports explicit skill lists, numeric general/scoped experience, selected CEFR language statements, country restrictions/authorization, sponsorship, office/student constraints and conservative education. Structured provider minimum experience retains scope; a seniority label does not become a mandatory numeric minimum. At most 200 requirements, including a mandatory truncation marker, and 1,000 characters per value/quote are retained.

Shared vacancy availability is restricted to OPEN/EXPIRED. Historical owner SAVED/APPLIED/INTERVIEW/REJECTED/OFFER/IGNORED states are sanitized on construction and deserialization, without rewriting stored history.

The engine reads one immutable manually saved search profile. These are self-reported statements, not independently verified résumé qualifications. A skill omitted from the profile is unconfirmed. General years do not establish professional or skill-specific years. Residence and relocation do not establish a permit. Optional ISO country authorization statements supply that evidence; a legacy global authorization enum supplies no jurisdiction. A no-degree statement does not establish the absence of a licence or professional certification. Education satisfaction requires an exact comparable statement; equivalences and ambiguous narratives stay UNKNOWN.

A confirmed mandatory incompatibility produces INELIGIBLE; missing or incomparable evidence produces UNKNOWN unless a blocker already exists. ELIGIBLE means the supported extracted hard requirements have positive evidence or are explicitly inapplicable. No score changes eligibility. Expiry is checked at query time without waiting for the worker.

Compatibility is a deterministic 0–100 score, never a hiring probability. Weights: role/title coverage 35; job-required/preferred skill coverage 35 (80/20 when both exist); comparable experience 15; geography, format, employment, industry and comparable salary preferences 3 each. Advertised versus saved seniority is a soft experience factor when numeric requirements are absent. Unknown factors receive a labelled half-value. Unrelated extra candidate skills cannot lower a score. Salary comparison requires identical currency, period and explicitly matching gross/net basis. No currency, annualization or net/gross conversion is invented.

Coverage measures established score dimensions discounted by unresolved hard checks. It is evidence availability, not confidence or probability. Reasons distinguish satisfied self-reported evidence, confirmed conflicts, unknowns and ranking preferences. INELIGIBLE history retains raw compatibility for audit but has no recommendation score; the UI suppresses its percentage. UNKNOWN remains conspicuous even with high compatibility. Ordering is eligibility group, compatibility descending, publication/first-seen descending, job ID descending; freshness never rewrites the score.

## Legacy catalogue admission

The original source discovery and Workday geographic facets remain unchanged. Valid normalized vacancies rejected by owner location/role/experience screening are now persisted once under the existing global identity/dedup rules. They have `legacy_admission_pending=true`, REJECT disposition, no legacy requirements/score, and no legacy notification eligibility. The owner review query and immediate/digest notifier also exclude pending rows. The original score and notification payload implementations remain in use.

When such a row later satisfies all original owner rules, admission clears the flag, runs the original extraction/scoring, and reports CREATED once to the unchanged ingestion notification path. Subsequent ordinary sightings do not become new deliveries. Matching bootstrap, profile changes and result backfill never write notification ledgers or call send services. Existing rows default to pending=false under V19. No production flag or behavior is changed by the local implementation.

## V19 and incremental work

V19 adds the admission flag, immutable vacancy fact revisions/current pointers, a commit-ordered catalogue change log, candidate-owned immutable results/current pointers and leased candidate work state. Composite FKs prevent a result/profile/current pointer from crossing candidate ownership. Full result identity includes candidate, vacancy, search-profile revision, fact revision, extractor and scorer versions. PostgreSQL triggers reject updates/deletes of immutable history. Existing job deletion is restricted once matching history exists. There is no purge or downgrade. V1–V18 and retained V17/V18 artifacts must remain byte-identical.

A profile activation and work-state reset share the profile transaction and candidate lock. Existing completed profiles and extractor/scorer upgrades initialize incrementally. Backfill captures the committed event watermark before the fact high-water job ID, scans bounded ID batches, then consumes later events. A global transaction-held catalogue row lock makes event allocation order also commit order, preventing missed late commits. Repeated events coalesce to the latest fact.

Claims use `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, a fencing token and lease. Calculation holds no publication locks. Publication locks candidate, work state, then fact-state rows in ascending job order, revalidates active completed profile, semantics, unexpired token and every fact identity, then atomically inserts results, updates pointers and advances progress. Obsolete work cannot advance a cursor or replace a newer generation. A crash before publication leaves a reclaimable lease; after commit it leaves complete results and progress. Failures back off five seconds to at most five minutes, stop after five attempts and expose FAILED. A new profile/semantics generation can deliberately restart work.

Defaults: matching disabled; batch25 (allowed1–100), bootstrap100 (1–500), one batch/tick, tick1,000ms (250–60,000), lease60sec (10–300), API default25/max50. Short worker transactions time out after10sec; publication lock wait3sec. Candidate/profile/fact payloads and source response envelopes remain bounded. Requests never compute candidate × catalogue. Recovery guards, worker and bootstrap independently prohibit recovery work.

## API and interface

All v2 routes use the authenticated session principal, revalidate admission/expiry and return `Cache-Control: no-store`. No candidate selector is accepted and no global score is substituted.

| Method/path | Response |
|---|---|
| GET `/api/mini-app/v2/me/matching` | Active profile and semantic versions, NOT_READY/PROCESSING/READY/FAILED state, publication/catalogue revision, bounded progress and processing flag |
| GET `/api/mini-app/v2/matches?limit=25&cursor=...&eligibility=RECOMMENDED` | Current owned vacancy/match/computation rows, nextCursor and state; filters RECOMMENDED, ELIGIBLE, UNKNOWN, INELIGIBLE, ALL |
| GET `/api/mini-app/v2/jobs/{jobId}` | Shared vacancy/facts, own workflow and current nullable matching projection; missing vacancy404 |

Only the exact matches GET route admits whitelisted scalar query parameters. Unknown, duplicate, oversized and malformed parameters fail400. Session-bound HMAC keyset cursors contain ordering and generation stamps, never an ownership selector. Foreign/malformed cursors fail400; a changed profile/publication/catalogue stamp fails409 MATCH_PAGE_STALE. Current reads require active completed profile, exact current fact and current extractor/scorer versions, plus a live vacancy.

Beta Catalogue remains the initial tab, with Recommended beside it. Recommended displays deterministic compatibility, coverage, typed reasons/source quotes, UNKNOWN warnings and a factor breakdown. Backfill shows processing or waiting without fabricated percentages. Stale responses clear the score. Paging replaces one bounded page. Open/detail/save/dismiss/applied use the existing private mutation/undo queue and respect pending/ambiguous writes. Credential-generation guards prevent old success, 401 or network completions from affecting a replacement account. Optional country authorization and salary basis require an explicit profile save. No credential is persisted in browser storage.

## Verification and capacity

The final private verification report is the authority for executed counts, source identity, migration/checkpoint comparisons, independent findings and closures, timings and image/archive checksums. Tests cover all20 acceptance cases in the approved request: cross-profile scores/eligibility/unknowns, languages/experience/location/authorization; retained global catalogue/dedup; API ownership/profile invalidation/stale publication; restart/bounded idempotent backfill; notification safety/legacy behavior; V13→V19 preservation and unchanged checksums; workflow/undo/applications; stable owned pagination; recovery silence.

`MatchingCapacityIT` uses disposable PostgreSQL limited to one CPU/512MiB, 50 profiles, 200 vacancies, including100 previously rejected rows. It measures ingestion delta/physical storage, 10,000 persisted evaluations in400 batches, latency/CPU/heap/WAL, one-profile200-job refresh, one-vacancy50-candidate fanout and the actual listing query plan with historical generations. A separate run bounds the JVM at512MiB/two visible CPUs. These measurements include synthetic workload overhead and are not Azure capacity certification. Retaining a vacancy costs one shared row/fact plus one result per candidate per relevant generation. Histories grow with profile/fact/semantic revisions; the implementation preserves them rather than hiding that cost through deletion.

At50 candidates×20,000 vacancies, initial backfill needs one million evaluations; at1,000 candidates,20 million. Default one25-result batch/second caps ideal drain at25 evaluations/second regardless of measured tight-loop throughput: about11.1hours or9.3days respectively, plus bootstrap/overhead. Fifty candidates×500 changed vacancies/day means25,000 evaluations/day;1,000 candidates means500,000/day, both arithmetic loads fit the ideal2.16million/day ceiling, before bootstrap, profile churn and transaction overhead. Sparse event batches can contain one result rather than25, so the ceiling is not guaranteed throughput. Change tick/batch ceilings only after measuring the actual deployment. Historical storage, WAL/backups and source update churn require operational budgeting before production.

## Limits and later work

Source retrieval still reflects the original Romania-oriented discovery/facets and finite source request/detail/deadline limits. Catalogue retention improves coverage only for vacancies already retrieved. No new crawl, AI extraction, résumé upload, automated applications, new infrastructure or LLM matching is included. Supported requirement grammar/country/city aliases are intentionally limited; unsupported content is UNKNOWN, not an inferred qualification. Structured salary ranges and legal equivalences are not resolved. User confirmations are self-reported. History retention grows storage; there is no automatic compaction or deletion policy. The singleton catalogue publication lock serializes fact changes; it is not a claim of unlimited ingestion throughput.

A V19-aware local recovery image must be verified and rehearsed after source freeze. The preserved V18 image must refuse committed V19; V19 must refuse unknown committedV20, unsafe recovery overrides, altered history and disabled Flyway. The actual-image rehearsal migrates synthetic V13, creates beta profiles/workflows/matches, fails a release, closes beta in recovery while owner workflows operate, compares full data fingerprints, resumes normal mode and runs an unstubbed mobile browser against the image. Existing evidence directories are never reused.

Phase5 plan only: extend the existing Telegram experience to each user with configurable compatibility thresholds, immediate alerts and optional daily digests. Use candidate-scoped delivery identities and explicit eligibility/coverage policy; historical/profile backfill must not flood notifications. Phase4 implements none of that. Stop here pending separate approval. Production remains NO-GO regardless of local verification until the separately approved Azure operational and release checks occur.

Neutral work format uses exact standalone positive worldwide-remote source clauses over legacy city-based inference. Explicit contradictory office/hybrid source evidence produces UNKNOWN plus a mandatory unresolved reason; it does not create an inferred office incompatibility. Independent confirmed restrictions still apply. The persisted legacy Job remote type and owner behavior remain unchanged.
